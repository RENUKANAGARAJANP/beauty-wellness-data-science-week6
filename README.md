# Week 6 – Model Evaluation, Reporting & Business Impact

## Project Title
**AI-Driven Personalization & Customer Retention System in Beauty & Wellness**

## Internship
YuvaIntern – Data Science with Python Analyst Internship

## Project Overview
This project focuses on using data science and machine learning to understand customer behaviour in the beauty and wellness industry. The main objective is to identify customers who may stop purchasing and support businesses in improving customer retention through personalized engagement strategies.

## Week 6 Objectives
- Evaluate the predictive models developed during Week 5.
- Compare model performance using classification metrics.
- Understand prediction errors using confusion matrices.
- Explain the business impact of model predictions.
- Provide actionable recommendations for customer retention.

## Models Evaluated
1. L2-Regularized Logistic Regression
2. Random Forest Classifier
3. Histogram Gradient Boosting

## Evaluation Metrics
- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-Score
- ROC-AUC
- PR-AUC
- Brier Score

## Key Results

| Model | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1-Score | ROC-AUC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Logistic Regression | 77.95% | 36.02% | 80.81% | 49.83% | 0.8648 |
| Random Forest | 80.40% | 39.14% | 80.44% | 52.66% | 0.8623 |
| Histogram Gradient Boosting | 87.75% | 59.03% | 31.37% | 40.96% | 0.8596 |

*Note: Results are based on the Week 5 repository benchmark data. They should not be interpreted as verified real-world business outcomes.*

## Business Recommendations
- Identify customers at risk of becoming inactive.
- Provide personalized product recommendations.
- Send timely replenishment reminders.
- Improve customer engagement through targeted retention campaigns.
- Monitor model performance and customer response over time.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Matplotlib
- Machine Learning

## Conclusion
This evaluation highlights how different machine learning models can support customer retention decisions. Model selection should consider the business objective, especially the balance between identifying at-risk customers and avoiding unnecessary retention offers.
